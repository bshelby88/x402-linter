Why payTo drift matters

A stale or wrong payTo silently misroutes every payment. Common failure modes: hardcoded constant diverges from the served 402 challenge, checksummed vs lowercase mismatch, wrong network (eip155:8453 = Base Mainnet), zero-value prices (facilitators reject $0 challenges — use charge-and-rebate instead).
